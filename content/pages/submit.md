# Submissions

Submissions to RLJ are accepted on an annual basis, adhering to a specific
submission deadline each year. The detailed submission guidelines and deadlines are
published in the annual Call for Papers of the Reinforcement Learning Conference
([RLC](https://rl-conference.cc/)), which is updated a few months prior to each
deadline and can be expected to fall around the same date each year.

## Review Process

Manuscript submission and peer review are managed through
[OpenReview](https://openreview.net/). Reviews are double-blind: submissions must be
anonymized, and identifying information (including in linked code) is grounds for
rejection. Each paper receives at least two reviewers supervised by an area chair,
with an author response phase after the initial reviews. The review process
prioritizes rigorous methodology over perceived importance. Reviews of rejected or
withdrawn papers are not made public.

## After Acceptance

Accepted papers are published open access in the current volume of the journal
under the Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY) license; authors retain copyright
over their work. Publication requires a signed
[Publication Agreement](https://rlj.cs.umass.edu/RLC_Publication_Agreement_Form.pdf).
One author of each accepted paper is expected to attend RLC and present the work.
